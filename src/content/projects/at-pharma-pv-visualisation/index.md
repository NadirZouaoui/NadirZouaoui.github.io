## Context

An installer was preparing a rooftop photovoltaic installation with battery storage for the At Pharma site, a pharmaceutical company's building. I was asked for the visualisation: images that show how the arrays, the inverters and the battery room would sit on the building, and how the modules are fixed to the roof.

The roof is made of sandwich panels. The storage room was drawn in two variants, with battery cabinets from two different manufacturers.

## What I did

My scope was visualisation only:

- 3D model of the building and its roof.
- Placement of the PV arrays and of the solar inverters on the roof.
- Layout of the technical room in two variants.
- Detailed model of the sandwich roof panel and of the module mounting system.
- Photorealistic renders and annotated views of these elements, built in Blender.

The module and inverter models follow the dimensions given in the manufacturers' datasheets supplied for the job.

## Result

The deliverable is a set of 17 images: overall views of the building with the arrays, close-ups of the roof, the inverter and the mounting, the two variants of the technical room, and annotated views with dimensions. The design, sizing and installation of the system were not part of my scope.
