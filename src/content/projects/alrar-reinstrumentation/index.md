## Context

In November 2018, Emerson, in partnership with Fores Engineering, signed a US$32 million contract with Sonatrach to modernise the Alrar gas processing complex: control and safety systems, fire and gas systems, field instrumentation, and control and isolation valves. Emerson and Fores handled engineering and procurement.

CEVIELEC SPA carried out the construction work on site, under Emerson's supervision. I worked there from March to October 2022, as Document Controller and on site.

## What I did

- Revision, modification and markup of project documents.
- Planning and progress tracking for the works.
- Cabinet wiring.
- Installation of field instruments.
- Loop checks and troubleshooting.
- Work on control valves: digital positioners replacing older pneumatic ones, with new brackets and feedback linkage.

## Result

The works were still in progress when my assignment ended in October 2022.
