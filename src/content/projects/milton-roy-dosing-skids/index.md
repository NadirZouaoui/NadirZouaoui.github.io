## Context

Milton Roy supplied corrosion inhibitor injection skids for gas wells in Algeria, for the end user Sonatrach. Each skid doses the inhibitor into the gas line with two metering pumps, one in service and one on standby, and stands in a Zone 2 area with an Ex d control box.

I was on site for PROSKID in 2024: 20 skids on the Alrar gas field and 7 solar-powered skids on a second field.

## What I did

- Inspection of each skid against the customer's checklist: earthing, supply polarity and voltage, wiring.
- Correction of the wiring and earthing faults found, including the link to the RTU.
- First filling and first start-up of the pumps, then relief valve setting and leak test at 110 % of the service pressure.
- Configuration of the instruments with a HART communicator, and communication tests with the RTU.
- Support for the customer's acceptance tests, site reports and a handover note.

## Result

27 skids worked on across the two fields. At Alrar, 17 of the 20 skids were made ready for commissioning, 12 passed the leak test at 110 % of the service pressure, and three passed the customer's acceptance tests without reserve.
