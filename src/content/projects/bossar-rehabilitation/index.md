## Context

The BOSSAR B-1600/D is a horizontal pouch packaging machine, built in 2005, that forms, fills and seals pouches. This one runs at GBFoods in Oran, on four product lines of the Jumbo brand, on a 380/400 V 50 Hz supply, and was controlled by an Omron PLC. Its original cabinet sat on top of the machine with the doors opening upwards, so conductive dust got in, clogged the components and short-circuited them.

The fix was to separate the electrical equipment from the dust. A new wall-mounted main enclosure, power and control, stands away from the machine, and a marshalling box with the HMI sits on top of the machine, with cable trays between the two.

## What I did

- Technical direction of the rehabilitation, from the site survey in February 2025.
- Wire-by-wire identification of the old cabinet before its removal in early March 2025.
- Redesign in EPLAN Electric P8: schematics, terminal and cable lists, with the panel layout in EPLAN Pro Panel.
- Hardware design of the new Siemens S7-1200 control (CPU 1215C with digital, relay, analog and thermocouple modules), with all I/O re-addressed from the old PLC.
- Panel build and wiring in the workshop, from April to May 2025.
- Supervision of the technicians during installation and rewiring on site, from late May to June 2025.
- Commissioning in July 2025: I directed the automation engineer, and the program was written and adjusted on the machine for the four product lines, above all the phase settings of the camshaft (angle counter).
- Troubleshooting during commissioning: the photocell that triggers the pouch cut, the brake, the vibrators, the motors and the suction cups.

## Result

The machine has been back in production since August 2025, on all four product lines.
